Customer Accepts the Quotation – Payment

The customer can complete the purchase using one of two payment methods.

Bank Transfer

  • • Email the customer the approved bank details, quotation and Terms & Conditions.
  • • Inform Accounting about the expected payment.
  • • Include the order number, customer number and amount to be paid.
  • • Accounting will confirm receipt of payment by email.
  • • Only then can you remove Operación por aclarar (operation to be clarified) so the order can be dispatched.

Payment by Link

  • • Change the order payment terms to PBYL.
  • • Inform the customer that the payment link is valid for 24 hours .
  • • Inform Accounting once the payment has been made.
  • • Include the order number, customer number and payment amount.

C) Checking Blocked Orders in SAP

Initial search – VA14L

Use SAP transaction VA14L to check for high-value blocked orders in Spain and Portugal.

Under Bloqueo de entrega cabecera (header delivery block) , first search using:

  • Delivery block: Z1
  • Sales organisation: 2090 (Spain)
  • Distribution channel: 01
  • Division: 01

Removing the block

  1. Open the order by clicking its number in the results table.
  2. In Bloqueo entrega (delivery block) , remove the Comprobación importe (amount check) option.
  3. Save the order.
  4. Close the order, return to the start page and click the green Save button.

Repeat the search for Z0 as well as Z1.

D) Accounting Emails – Blocked Customers

Daily accounting reports

We receive automated accounting report emails every day. Only two reports require action:

  • Customers who must be blocked because they entered false or incorrect order details, breaching the Terms and Conditions.
  • Cancellation and credit-note reports for orders returned to Germany.

Archive the other reports in the Accounting folder in Outlook.

Blocking a customer

  1. Open the report email and review the attached PDF listing the customers to block.
  2. If the message “Error en el bloque de la Contraparte” appears, email the Accounting team to request the customer blocks.
  3. Email the affected customers to inform them that their accounts have been disabled.
  4. Keep the previous day’s sent email in the Accounting folder marked as unread. Forward it with all customers and SAC in BCC so it is easy to locate the following day.

D) Cancellation and Refund Reports

Verify the cancellation

  1. Open the cancellation/refund report from Germany.
  2. In SAP, run transaction VA05 for the customer number.
  3. Extend the search date to include the previous two months.
  4. Check whether the customer has the relevant ZTAI order and RE return created by Germany.

Decide what to report to Accounting

  • If the ZTAI order and RE return are correct and no earlier compensation exists, forward the report to Accounting and request the refund.
  • If a ZTA3 exists with the same contents and the free replacement has been dispatched, this refund is not appropriate.
  • If the claim shipment has already been sent, tell Accounting not to refund the affected customer ; issue an L2 invoice to compensate the account.

For the L2 process, see J) Rectificación abonos – Facturas L2 (and section 37) and record it in MARKETING\ABONOS CONTABILIDAD_01 .

L2 invoices – correcting a credit note

When to use L2

L2 corrects an incorrect or duplicate credit note so the customer receives only the appropriate refund.

If a free ZTA3 replacement with the same contents was dispatched, tell Accounting not to refund and follow the L2 correction procedure. Record the correction in ABONOS CONTABILIDAD_01.

Documented sequence

1. In Crear pedido (create order), select L2. Choose Crear con referencia (create with reference) and enter the original purchase invoice.

2. Choose Tomar (copy). Set Motivo de pedido (order reason) to Razón devolución desconocida (unknown return reason), then save.

3. Record the generated document number. Open it in Modificar pedido (change order) and save to obtain the invoice number. Coordinate the correction with Accounting.

Source: January 2026 manual, pages 27–29; 9

Demandware – an order is missing from SAP

Investigate the order

Use advanced search in Demandware / Salesforce and search by the customer’s email to review their order history.

ERROR and CREADO orders were not recorded correctly and cannot be processed or dispatched. Check whether the customer is blocked in SAP or whether billing details differ between earlier orders.

Explain and escalate

If the account is blocked, explain that the order cannot be dispatched. If there is a system error without an account block, offer a manual quotation and payment by transfer or link.

Financial handling is unresolved in the manual: page 36 requests a refund, while page 37 describes an authorization hold. Ask Accounting to verify the actual payment status; do not promise a refund or its timing.

Manual recovery mentions website conditions, but the newer training excludes promotions on email orders. Escalate any promotion exception before promising it.

Source: January 2026 manual, pages 36–37; October 2026 training, pages 16

Reactivating a discount code

Check the specific situation

A single-use code may become inactive after an attempted order that was not confirmed. This procedure is for that documented situation.

In Demandware, open Pedidos (orders) and search using the customer’s email. Review the order history.

Reactivate and save

Open the latest relevant order marked ERROR or CREADO.

In ESTADO DE PEDIDO (order status), select error and save. This reactivates the code in the documented case.

Do not apply this procedure to a completed purchase or promise that a code is eligible for every product. Check the promotion conditions.

Source: January 2026 manual, pages 38

Order validation and operational exceptions

Validate according to payment method

Bank transfer: wait for Accounting confirmation, then open Modificar pedido (change order), remove Operación por aclarar and save.

Payment link (PBYL): validation is automatic. Do not remove Operación por aclarar manually. Restore the block after editing an unpaid manual order.

Check the exceptions

Claims usually cannot be changed after saving. A change may still be possible immediately after saving, before the delivery document is processed.

A customer number beginning 130 belongs to a shop (B2B); do not use it for a consumer claim. Check for the appropriate consumer account before creating a new one.

For a blocked customer requiring a credit note, ask Accounting to remove the block temporarily and to restore it afterwards. Surprise-bag spare parts cannot be ordered.

Webshops are B2C for Spain and Portugal. Handle consumer claims submitted through shops; route other B2B claims to Sales Administration.

Source: January 2026 manual, pages 14, 22, 24, 31, 38; October 2026 training, pages 3, 28–30
